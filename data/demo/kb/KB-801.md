# Getting started with the PLM for new engineers

New engineers take the two-hour **PLM Basics** course in the learning portal, then the **ECO for Engineers** course. Your team's PLM champion can pair with you for your first ECO. The quick reference for part search, ECOs, and the vault is attached to this article.
