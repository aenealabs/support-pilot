# Why an ECO was returned and how to resubmit

A returned ECO goes back to the originator in the **Rework** stage with the reviewer's comment on the **Route** tab.

1. Read the comment and fix what it asks for: usually affected parts, cost impact, drawings, or effectivity.
2. Choose **Resubmit**. The ECO restarts at Technical Review.

Do not create a new ECO for the same change: it loses the review history.
