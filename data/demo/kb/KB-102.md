# Check in a large assembly without timeouts

Check-ins of assemblies with more than 2,000 components can time out over VPN.

1. Close every drawing that references the assembly.
2. In the vault client choose **Check in > Selected files only** and check in the changed parts first, then the top assembly.
3. If you are on VPN, use the **Remote Site cache**: set **Tools > Options > Site** to your nearest site before checking in.

If a check-in still fails, the client log is in **Help > Show log folder**; attach `vault-client.log` to a ticket.
