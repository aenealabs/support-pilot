# A released BOM did not reach SAP

Released BOMs transfer to SAP in a nightly batch at 01:00 plant time, so a BOM released during the day appears in SAP the next morning.

If it has not arrived after the nightly run:
1. In the PLM, open the part and choose **Integration > Transfer status**.
2. If the status is **Failed**, the message shows the first error, most often a material that does not exist in the receiving plant.
3. Open a ticket in the **PLM-SAP Integration** queue with the part number, the plant, and the error text.

Do not re-release the BOM to force a transfer; it creates a new revision.
