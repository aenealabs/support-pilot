# Audit log

Every question, review, draft decision, hand-off, import, and refused attempt is recorded
with who did it and when. Only approvers can open the log.

## Searching

Search by words (including keys such as `PRB-3` or `KB-12`) and filter by category:
questions and answers, tickets and problems, and sign-in and access.

## Refused attempts

Recorded as **access denied**: someone who is not a support lead requesting a review or draft,
deciding a draft, sending to Backlog Pilot, importing tickets, or opening this log; and an
action tried without signing in. A sign-in that fails is recorded as **sign-in failed**.
