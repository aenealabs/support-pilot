# Find where your ECO is in approval

Open the ECO and choose the **Route** tab. The current step shows the stage (Technical Review, Change Board, or Implementation), who holds it, and since when.

If it has been in one stage for more than 10 business days, use **Route > Send reminder**. If the approver is away, their delegate is shown under **Delegations**; the change coordinator can reassign the step.
