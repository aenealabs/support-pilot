# Data

The knowledge base and tickets the app works from.

## Tickets

Support leads import tickets (a CSV export from the service desk) on this page; problems are
recomputed from them. Every import is audited.

## Where the data comes from

Tickets come from the service desk (for example ServiceNow) and articles from the knowledge
base; Support Pilot only reads them. Approved draft articles are added to the knowledge base.
The demo uses fictional articles and tickets.
