# Create and submit an ECO in the PLM

1. In the PLM, choose **New > Engineering Change Order** and pick the change type: design change, document change, part obsolescence, or supplier change.
2. Add every affected part, not only the top assembly. Use **Add > From where-used** to bring in child parts and fasteners.
3. Fill in the **Cost impact** section, including scrap and rework of existing stock.
4. Attach the updated drawings at the new revision, not markups.
5. Set the effectivity date after any SAP cutover window.
6. Choose **Submit for technical review**.

Most ECOs that come back are missing affected parts or cost impact.
